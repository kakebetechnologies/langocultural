who we are?

Preserving Lango Culture,
Uniting Our People

The Lango Cultural Institution (LCI) is the traditional authority representing the Lango people of mid-northern Uganda. Under the leadership of Won Nyaci Eng. Dr. Moses Michael Odongo Okune, we serve millions of people across multiple districts including Lira, Apac, Alebtong, Dokolo, Amolatar, Oyam, Kole, Otuke, and Kwania. With 149 registered clans, our mandate encompasses preserving Lango cultural heritage, promoting unity among our people, and supporting sustainable community development through education, cultural programs, and strategic partnerships with government and development organizations.

LCI provides a unifying platform for clan leadership, cultural institutions, and development partners to collaborate on practical solutions that strengthen social cohesion and improve livelihoods. We coordinate community dialogues, preserve oral history, safeguard traditional values, and promote responsible cultural tourism so that heritage remains relevant in modern life. Through inclusive participation, transparent leadership, and long-term planning, we continue to protect the dignity and identity of the Lango people while building a resilient future for every household across the sub-region.

Lango Cultural Pillars

The foundational elements that define and preserve our Lango identity across generations

149 Clans United

Our strength lies in the unity of all Lango clans, each preserving unique histories while contributing to our collective identity. From the Atyak to the Ucucu, every clan plays a vital role in our cultural tapestry.

Leb Lango Language

Our language is the vessel of our wisdom, proverbs, and oral traditions. Through digital tools, education programs, and community initiatives, we ensure Leb Lango thrives in the modern world.

Traditional Arts

From the energetic Okeme dance to the rhythmic Abuda ceremonies, our artistic expressions connect us to our ancestors. We document, teach, and celebrate these traditions through festivals and workshops.

Youth Empowerment

We invest in our future by providing scholarships, leadership training, and cultural education to Lango youth, ensuring they carry our heritage forward with pride and purpose.

Environmental Stewardship

Our "Greening Lango" initiative promotes sustainable practices, tree planting, and environmental conservation, honoring our ancestors' respect for the land that sustains us.

Digital Heritage Archive

We are building a comprehensive digital archive of clan histories, elder testimonies, traditional knowledge, and cultural artifacts to preserve our heritage for future generations.
